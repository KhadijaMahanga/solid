## The problem

Organizing an event in Tanzania often works through group contributions: guests contribute money toward the event, and the amount they give determines what kind of invitation they receive — a single card, a double card, or none yet. Tracking this by hand across dozens or hundreds of guests — who has paid, how much, what card they're owed, and whether they've been sent it — is tedious and easy to get wrong.

## What we're building

Mwaliko is a mobile app (iOS, Android, and web, built with Expo/React Native) that manages this end-to-end:

- **Event & guest management** — organizers create an event and add guests, including importing directly from phone contacts.
- **Contribution tracking** — payments are recorded against each guest, with configurable thresholds (e.g. TZS 50,000 for a single card, TZS 100,000 for a double card).
- **Automatic card assignment** — a database trigger updates each guest's card type and status the moment a payment is recorded, so there's no manual step to remember.
- **WhatsApp/SMS delivery** — invitation cards and reminders are sent straight to guests through Africa's Talking messaging API.

## Status

Mwaliko is in active development and not yet publicly released. It will be launched here once available.
