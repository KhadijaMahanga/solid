## The problem

Eight local media outlets, based in communities along Tanzania's Kwakuchinja, Nyerere Selous–Udzungwa, and Amani Nilo corridors, needed a professional, reliable way to publish news online. None of them had an in-house technical team, so whatever was built had to be run day-to-day by editorial staff, not developers.

## What we built

Over an 8-week engagement, we designed, built, and deployed a WordPress-based news platform for each of the 8 outlets:

- **Responsive, fast-loading sites** with custom post types and multiple page layouts, built to load quickly even on slower connections.
- **Editor-friendly CMS** - content managers can add or change menus, pages, tabs, links, images, and social media feeds themselves, and manage admin and content-editor user roles, without routine developer involvement.
- **Reporter bylines** - stories can be published under a reporter's real name or anonymously as "By our reporter," giving newsrooms flexibility on sensitive stories.
- **Live radio streaming** embedded directly on the site for outlets with a radio component.
- **Search and discovery** by news category, topic, writer, resource type, event, or region, plus a newsletter sign-up for latest news.
- **Social integration** - social sharing including Twitter share-by-text-selection, a live Twitter feed by handle or hashtag, and integrated maps, email, and contact forms.
- **Visitor analytics** - traffic trends, time spent on content, and audience demographics (including gender) to help each newsroom understand and improve how their content performs.
- **Security, backups, and compliance** - scheduled backups, regular security patching, and cookie/consent handling aligned with anti-spam and data-protection standards.

## Results

Each outlet was handed a fully tested, independently manageable news website, along with staff training, English-language user manuals, and the complete source code - so day-to-day publishing and content updates don't depend on us.
